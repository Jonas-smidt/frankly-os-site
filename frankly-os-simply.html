For everyone at Frankly

Frankly OS is our shared brain.

When one of us learns something, all of us know it. That's the whole idea.

A real example

Anyone on the team can now make images the way Jonas does.

Jonas spent many rounds learning what a Frankly image needs: light and positive, real Copenhagen places, life being lived. Every one of those lessons went into the system. Now a teammate can just ask for a lifestyle photo and get one in the same style.

It's only as good as what we've used it for.

What it can do today is simply the work it has done so far, mostly images, copy and pitches. When you use it for your kind of task, it learns that skill too, and then everyone has it.

How do I use it?

Three things to remember.

Just describe the work

Say what you need in plain words. You don't pick a helper; it finds the right one.

Tell it what you learned

If something worked or went wrong, say "log this as feedback". That's how your lesson becomes everyone's.

Use Cowork or Claude Code

That's where the specialist helpers live. The plain Chat tab has the recipes but not the helpers.

What problem does it solve?

Before, everyone learned alone.

How does it work?

A loop that gets smarter every time.

You ask for something in plain words. The system does the rest, and it remembers what it learned.

Where is it going?

The more we use it, the smarter it gets.

Like a snowball: every job adds a little. Today one person teaches it the most. Soon all of us will.

Still asks first

Three words you'll hear

Think of it as a family kitchen.

Routes = recipes

The recipe book

A step-by-step way to make one kind of thing: a pitch, a lifestyle photo, a blog post.

Specialists = helpers

The cooks

Each helper is good at one job: writing, images, research, checking quality.

Lessons = notes in the margin

"Less salt next time"

After every job, we write down what worked and what didn't, right inside the recipe.

The tech words, made simple

Skill, plugin, connector, agent, crew.

Skill

A how-to card. Claude picks it up by itself when your task matches, e.g. "how we make a partner pitch".

Like a recipe card

Plugin

A package you install once. It brings a whole bundle of skills and helpers onto your computer in one go.

Like a lunchbox full of recipe cards

Connector

A cable into another app, like Drive, Notion, Figma or Slack, so Claude can look at what's in there.

Like a plug in the wall

Agent

A Claude that gets one job, goes off and does it on its own, then comes back with the result.

Like a helper sent on one errand

Frankly crew

Our own team of 22 agents, each with a speciality, plus a coordinator who hands out the jobs and puts the answers together.

Like the kitchen team and its head chef
