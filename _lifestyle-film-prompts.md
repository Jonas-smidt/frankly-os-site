# Frankly lifestyle films — Higgsfield batch 01 (2026-10-03)

Internal Lab material. Ten 8-second lifestyle films generated in Higgsfield (Seedance 2.5,
720p, native ambient audio, no dialogue, no text, no logos). Rendered files live in the
Higgsfield account under the job IDs below; nothing here is cleared for public use.

## Brief (grounded in `frankly-brand-reference.html` § 04 Photography)

- Life first. Insurance somewhere behind it. No product, no claim, no copy on screen.
- Observed, not staged. Lived-in Copenhagen places, candid energy, people absorbed in their own world.
- One person · one action · one colour beat (dusty pink, bordeaux, mustard, cobalt, bottle green).
- Warm street light or warm home light, tactile detail, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field.
- Ambient sound only. The ribbon is left out: it has no spatial job in live-action footage.
- Themes lifted from the journal: bike, home is not a museum, a Tuesday when something breaks,
  take the good gear travelling, repair rather than replace, first weeks with something new,
  one thing less to think about, kids using the good things.

## Prompts

### 01 · Harbour bike, golden hour (16:9)
Candid documentary lifestyle film, Copenhagen harbour quay at golden hour. A single woman in her 30s in a dusty-pink wool coat rides a pale pink city e-bike along the cobblestones beside the water, old sailboats and colourful townhouses behind her, low sun flaring off the harbour. Camera: smooth lateral tracking shot at hip height following her at bike speed, shallow depth of field, slight natural handheld drift. Lighting: warm low backlight, long shadows across the cobbles. She glances at the water and smiles to herself. Ambient: bike tyres on cobbles, harbour gulls, distant cafe chatter.

### 02 · Sofa is not a museum (16:9, retake)
Warm Copenhagen apartment, lived-in living room with mustard-yellow walls, a dark green velvet sofa and a large monstera by a tall window with red-brick rooftops outside. A single girl about 7 in a striped jumper bounces happily up and down on the sofa cushions, hair flying, while her father sits on the floor beside the coffee table watching her and laughing, a laptop and two ceramic mugs on the table. Camera: static medium-wide at child eye level, both people fully in frame, then a very slow push-in. Lighting: bright soft afternoon window light. Ambient: the girl giggling, sofa springs, city hum.

First take (job 42c7142c-fe64-46c1-aa9e-0ed88fbfae76) used two children who "bounce and tumble"; mid-clip the two bodies merged into one shape. Retake limits the scene to one child and one action.

### 03 · Tuesday, phone on the pavement (16:9)
Copenhagen side street on a grey Tuesday morning, wet cobblestones, pastel yellow and rose facades. A single man in his late 20s in a bottle-green varsity jacket bends down to pick his smartphone up from the pavement where it lies face down, turns it over, checks the screen, gives a small relieved shrug and keeps walking. Camera: medium shot, eye level, gentle handheld. Lighting: soft overcast daylight with a warm shop-window glow. Calm, not dramatic. Ambient: footsteps on wet stone, a bicycle bell, light rain dripping.

### 04 · Train window, camera in lap (16:9)
Inside a Danish regional train. A single woman in her 20s in a cobalt-blue knit sweater sits by the window with a compact mirrorless camera in her lap, headphones on, watching flat green coastal fields and the sea slide past. Sunlight flickers across her face through passing trees. Camera: medium close-up from the seat opposite, static with subtle train vibration, window reflections. Ambient: train rhythm on rails, muffled announcement tone, soft wind.

### 05 · New ear cushion (16:9)
Small Copenhagen kitchen with sage-green cabinets and a wooden table. A single man in his 40s with grey stubble carefully fits a new fabric ear cushion onto a pair of over-ear headphones, a small screwdriver and the worn old cushion beside him, coffee steaming. Camera: medium shot across the table, static locked-off, focus on hands and face, slow deliberate movement. Lighting: soft north window light. Mustard-yellow bowl as the colour beat. Ambient: fabric rustle, a radio murmuring in another room, kettle ticking.

### 06 · First espresso (16:9, retake)
Copenhagen kitchen with limestone-white walls and warm oak counter. A single woman in her 30s in an oversized cream shirt makes her first espresso on a brand-new compact espresso machine in matte blush-pink enamel with a completely plain smooth front panel, generic unbranded design with only a chrome portafilter and a small steam wand, steam rising as coffee pours into a small ceramic cup, she leans in to smell it and smiles. Camera: slow push-in from medium to close-up, soft handheld. Lighting: low golden morning sun through a side window. Ambient: espresso hiss, cup set down, birds outside. No lettering anywhere, no brand names.

First take (job 786863cb-da43-4785-9607-d82adaf78989) rendered a real appliance brand name on the machine ("retro espresso machine" pulls that design in). Retake says "plain smooth front panel, generic unbranded" and bans lettering explicitly.

### 07 · Phone face down, tea (16:9)
Copenhagen home, evening. A single mother in her late 30s in a bordeaux-red cardigan leans against the kitchen counter after bedtime, places her smartphone face down, exhales slowly, wraps both hands around a warm mug of tea and looks out of the dark window at the city lights. A child's drawing on the fridge, a scooter helmet on a hook. Camera: medium close-up, three-quarter profile, very slow drift. Lighting: single warm pendant lamp, cool blue night outside. Ambient: fridge hum, distant tram, mug set down.

### 08 · Flower street, blue hour (9:16)
Copenhagen neighbourhood street at blue hour, string lights over cafe tables, a flower shop with buckets of pink dahlias on the pavement. A couple in their 30s walk arm in arm toward camera, she in a long dusty-pink wool coat with a small paper bag, he in a dark green varsity jacket laughing at something she said. Camera: medium shot, slow backward tracking at their pace. Lighting: warm shop-window and string-light glow against cool dusk, pastel facades. Ambient: footsteps on cobbles, cafe murmur, a bicycle passing.

### 09 · Headphones dance (9:16)
A single girl about 7 in a cobalt-blue knit sweater dances freely in a Copenhagen living room wearing her parent's big over-ear headphones, eyes closed, arms swinging, on a worn wooden floor with a dusty-pink rug, a record player and a stack of books on a low shelf, afternoon sun through tall windows. Camera: medium-wide at child eye level, static with slight handheld breathing. Ambient: music leaking from headphones, socks on wooden floor, birds outside.

### 10 · Cargo bike morning (9:16)
Copenhagen morning. A single father in his 30s in a mustard-yellow rain jacket pedals a black cargo bike with two small children in the front box wearing bright helmets, past pastel townhouses and a bakery with its lights on, morning cyclists behind. Camera: lateral tracking shot at child height, then a gentle pan as it passes. Lighting: pale sun breaking through after rain, wet asphalt reflections. Ambient: cargo bike rattle, children chatting indistinctly, bakery door bell.

## Lessons

- Seedance 2.5 recommends the "IN THE DARK" preset for any evening or child-at-home prompt and refuses the job; pass `declined_preset_id` on those items up front.
- "Retro espresso machine" (and similar design-era words) pulls a real brand mark onto the object. Describe the object as plain and unbranded and ban lettering explicitly.
- Two children doing one physical action together is the merge risk. One person, one action is also the brand rule, so keep it.
- Ten 8-second films at 720p cost 560 credits; budget two retakes per batch of ten.
