## Location blocks (block 4 of the six-block prompt; one line each)

### 1 · Mud (NO CARD, unverified)
A narrow singletrack trail through a Danish beech forest in late autumn on flat, gently rolling ground: smooth pale-grey beech trunks rising straight and close together, moss buildup at their bases, a floor of copper-brown fallen leaves, black wet earth churned into deep ruts holding standing brown water, pale roots crossing the trail, thin bare saplings in the understorey, the canopy half bare so the sky shows only in pieces between the crowns, and far down the trail a second rider's small red rear light and a walker with a dog on a lead.
Tells: hills or mountains; golden Mediterranean light or deep blue sky; conifer forest instead of beech.
Sky: Soft overcast Copenhagen light, broad and even, with no visible sun and no hard shadows; the sky showing between the crowns is dense grey rather than blown-out white.

### 2 · Puddle (cards: bike-infrastructure, street-furniture, facades-and-sky, jaegersborggade grammar)
An ordinary Nørrebro side street just after rain: continuous five-storey street walls with flat straight cornices, dark red-brick blocks with white timber windows beside plastered fronts in pale yellow, grey and cream, a roadway of wet granite setts, a one-directional cycle track of plain dark-grey asphalt raised one granite kerb step above the setts and one step below a pavement of large concrete flags with single rows of small granite cobbles, black conical lamps hanging on wires across the street, cars parked along the kerb, bikes leaned against the walls, water-staining under the sills, and a rain puddle lying in a dip of the track.
Flag: setts plus a raised track is a less common pairing; if frames read as a set, change "granite setts" to "asphalt".
Tells: a red or green Dutch lane or a painted line instead of a raised track; rounded movie-set cobbles; lamp posts on the pavement instead of wire-hung lamps.
Sky: Soft overcast light after rain, the sky a dense grey strip above the cornices, never blown-out white; the wet setts and track reflect the grey and the warm windows.

### 3 · Road bike (NO CARD, canon grammar)
A wide flat common on the edge of Copenhagen: pale dry grass and reeds to knee height, a straight compacted pale-grey gravel track running to the horizon, a single line of low willows along one side, no buildings near, a completely flat horizon where only a thin low band of distant trees and the faint grey line of the city sit, open sky filling two-thirds of the frame, a dog walker small and far off on the track.
Tells: hills, palms or cypresses; golden Mediterranean sunlight; lycra racing cliché (keep understated).
Sky: Sunny Danish afternoon sky with visible soft cumulus clouds, their lower edges glowing pale warm pink and peach where the low sun catches them from below, deeper soft grey-blue toward the upper sky. Clouds clearly defined and structured, not blown out into white. Sun entirely out of frame.

### 4 · BMX (NO CARD, canon grammar)
An ordinary public concrete skatepark set in a Copenhagen park lawn: smooth pale-grey poured-concrete transitions and a quarter pipe with a steel coping lip, hairline cracks and dark rain-staining in the flat bottom, a low plain concrete ledge, worn grass around the edges, mature lime and chestnut trees, a row of galvanised hoop racks with two city bikes leaned in, two kids waiting on the deck with boards under their arms, and beyond the trees a low even line of five-storey brick and plaster facades on a flat horizon.
Tells: a skyscraper or glass tower behind; deep blue Mediterranean sky; readable or gibberish lettering on the concrete (graffiti left out on purpose).
Sky: as scene 3.

### 5 · Night ride (cards: facades-and-sky, street-furniture, bike-infrastructure, movia-bus)
An ordinary five-storey residential street in Nørrebro at night: continuous street walls of dark red brick and grey and ochre plaster with white-framed windows, a few lit warm behind curtains, ground-floor shopfronts dark except one small kiosk window glowing, black conical lamps hanging on steel wires in a line down the middle of the street and throwing round pools onto damp asphalt, a plain dark-grey cycle track one granite kerb step above the road, bikes leaned against the walls, a dark-green cylindrical bin on a post, and behind the riders a plain egg-yolk-yellow single-deck electric bus with a black window band and a lit interior sliding past.
Tells: a red double-decker or turquoise bus; the LED destination strip as a near-word (keep the bus side-on and soft); Victorian lamp posts or LED poles instead of wire-hung cones.
Sky: The brightest points in the frame are the wire-hung lamps and the bus's lit windows; the sky above the cornices is a dark blue-grey with low cloud faintly lit from below by the city, never black and never orange.

### 6 · Date at the lake (cards: dronning-louises-bro lake-edge facts, street-furniture, facades-and-sky)
The grass bank of a still, shallow city lake in Copenhagen seen from low down: short worn grass sloping to a low stone edge, flat calm water holding the sky's reflection, a gravel path behind under a row of mature lime and chestnut trees, a dark-green bench with cast-iron scroll-arm ends and wide wooden slats on the path, swans and gulls on the water, a jogger and a pram passing on the path, and the far shore closed off by a continuous line of tree crowns with only glimpses of low plaster facades between the trunks, on a completely flat horizon.
Tells: the lake drawn as a river with a current; a teak or white park bench; glass towers or tall blocks on the far shore.
Sky: Warm amber light, low and directional, with the sun entirely out of frame; soft structured cumulus with pale warm pink and peach lower edges reflected in the still water, deeper grey-blue above, no blown-out white.

### 7 · Morning stream (cards: bike-infrastructure, street-furniture, facades-and-sky, movia-bus)
A busy ordinary Nørrebro street on a weekday morning: five-storey dark red-brick blocks with white timber windows, plastered ground-floor shopfronts with small dark fabric awnings, a one-directional cycle track of plain dark-grey asphalt running with the traffic, raised one granite kerb step above the asphalt road and one step below a pavement of concrete flags with rows of granite cobbles, trees in square granite-edged pits between track and road, a short blue-painted cycle crossing through the junction ahead, a small bicycle traffic signal, a plain galvanised steel footrest rail at the stop line, galvanised hoop racks full of bikes, lamps on wires over the road, and a yellow city bus at the corner.
Tells: blue paint along the whole lane; a two-way track on one side; the footrest rail's printed message as gibberish (hence "plain").
Sky: Pale morning strip above the rooftops, soft structured cloud, grey-blue, never white; low warm side light from the left, the sun entirely out of frame below the roofline, long soft shadows across the track.
